Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026
Short version: ZonProbe runs entirely in your browser. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any of your data. Nothing you analyze is ever sent to us or to any third party.

1. What ZonProbe does

ZonProbe is a browser extension that analyzes product reviews displayed on Amazon product pages. It reads text already present on the page you are viewing and produces a local analysis report (sentiment split, frequently praised/criticized terms, rating distribution, and representative quotes).

2. Data we collect

None. ZonProbe does not collect personal information, does not require an account, and does not use analytics or tracking of any kind.

3. How your data is processed

  • All analysis happens locally in your browser, on your device.
  • Page content (review text, ratings) is read on the page and processed in memory. It is never uploaded to a server.
  • ZonProbe makes no network requests to transmit your data. There is no backend server.

4. What is stored on your device

ZonProbe stores a single preference locally using the browser's extension storage:

  • Your interface language choice (English or Simplified Chinese).

This stays on your device and is never transmitted. You can remove it at any time by uninstalling the extension or clearing the extension's storage.

5. Permissions and why they are needed

  • activeTab / scripting - to read the review content on the Amazon page you are currently viewing, only when you click "Analyze".
  • storage - to remember your language preference locally.
  • sidePanel - to display the analysis report in the browser's side panel.
  • Host permissions (Amazon domains) - so the extension can operate on Amazon product and review pages.

6. Third parties

ZonProbe does not share data with any third party, because it does not transmit data at all. There are no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services embedded in the extension.

7. Children's privacy

ZonProbe is a tool for professional and personal use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
